Privacy Notice & UK GDPR Compliance

        UK Data Protection Act 2018 · Transparent, Local & Private
      

      
    

    

      

        
1. Data Minimisation & Local Storage Architecture

        
Bet Buddy is built on a Privacy-by-Design principle. Your Bet Diary, accumulator slips, private custom odds, and settings are stored locally on your device via browser localStorage. We do not transmit or store your personal betting slips on remote proprietary databases without your explicit permission.

      

      

        
2. No Third-Party Tracking Cookies

        
We do not operate advertising tracking pixels or third-party behavioral analytics that track you across the internet. Essential operational state (such as fractional/decimal preferences and competition filters) is maintained client-side only.

      

      

        
3. Optional Google Authentication

        
Signing in with Google is optional. We only access your basic public profile (display name and email address) to synchronize your Buddy League gamertag. You can continue using Bet Buddy 100% anonymously at any time.

      

      

        
4. Your Rights Under UK GDPR (Articles 15–20)

        
    
          
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export your full Bet Diary at any time in open CSV or JSON format using the Export buttons in the Bet Diary.
    • 
          
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Tap "Clear Diary" in the Bet Diary to permanently delete every stored bet from your device instantly.
    • 
          
  • Right of Rectification: Update your username or correct any bet entries directly inside the app.
    • 
        

      

      

        
5. External Bookmaker Outbound Links

        
When you choose to click "Place Bet" or visit an external licensed bookmaker (such as bet365, Sky Bet, Paddy Power, Ladbrokes, Coral, William Hill, or Betfair), you leave Bet Buddy. Any data processed on bookmaker platforms is governed by their respective UKGC licenses and privacy policies. Bet Buddy may receive affiliate commissions from qualifying bookmaker referrals at zero cost to you.
